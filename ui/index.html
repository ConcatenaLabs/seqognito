Seqognito

A mixing wallet for Sequentia. Everything it does goes over Tor.

Your recovery phrase

Twelve words. They are the wallet: anyone who has them has the coins, and nobody can give them back to you. Write them down on paper, off this machine.

Encrypts the phrase in a file on this computer. It is not part of the recovery phrase and it cannot be recovered — forget it and you restore from the twelve words instead.

Where should this wallet connect?

Seqognito talks to nodes you choose, and only through Tor. Point it at your own Sequentia and Bitcoin Esplora endpoints (an .onion address is ideal — nothing outside Tor ever sees the request), or at ones you trust.

Receive

The same address receives parent-chain Bitcoin and Sequentia assets — on Sequentia the transparent address is byte-identical to a Bitcoin one. A blinded address is the opt-in private form; only Sequentia assets can be sent to it.

—

Send

Ordinary payments. Nothing here is private by itself — a payment straight out of a mixed coin tells the recipient which mixed coin is yours.

Mix

Your coins go into one transaction with other people's, and come out somewhere nobody can follow. On Sequentia the amounts are hidden too — every output of the round, your change included, is a blinded commitment — so there are no equal-value denominations on the chain to count and no change output to spot.

Your coins and your mixed addresses are registered over different Tor circuits, each rotated before use. That separation is the reason this is a desktop application: the coordinator sees two unrelated strangers instead of one IP address twice.
